ForeSeeMe

The Liquid Glass Healthcare System

A single, coherent visual language for an AI-powered environmental disease-risk app. It evolves the original futuristic identity — deep space shell, brand gradient, glass cards — with the discipline of Material 3, the restraint of Liquid Glass, and the calm of premium medical design.

14 Principles

Accessibility Guidelines

Never color alone

Every risk & status signal pairs color with a label and a unique icon shape (✓ ! △ ?). Verified against deuteranopia & protanopia.

Contrast ≥ 4.5:1

Body text on glass meets WCAG AA. Glass fills sit over a fixed dark shell so contrast never collapses on bright content behind.

≥ 44px targets

All interactive elements meet a 44×44px minimum hit area, including nav icons and chips, with visible focus rings.

Reduced motion

Honors prefers-reduced-motion: ambient breathing & float stop, transitions shorten to a simple fade.
